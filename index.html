What happens to each document

Clients keep using email or a simple upload link. Nothing changes for them.

  1. It arrives

    Clients email files or upload them through a private link. Photos, scans, and PDFs all work, even the crooked ones.

  2. It gets recognized

    DeskTask identifies the document type, such as a driver's license, declarations page, loss run, or certificate request, and matches it to the client and policy.

  3. It gets filed and chased

    The document lands in the right client folder with a clear name. If something's missing, DeskTask asks the client for it on a schedule you set.

You stay in charge

What DeskTask does

  • Sorts and names incoming documents
  • Matches them to the right client and policy
  • Tracks what's still missing per client
  • Sends reminders in your voice

What it never does

  • Quote, bind, or change a policy
  • Give coverage advice
  • Send anything to a carrier without your approval
  • Replace your management system. It works beside it

Client data stays yours

Handled carefully

  • Encrypted when sent and when stored
  • Original files are deleted automatically 30 days after filing; the extracted details stay searchable
  • Your clients' files are never used to train AI models

Questions agencies ask

Do I have to switch software?

No. DeskTask works alongside whatever you use now. Filed documents can be downloaded into your existing system at any time.

Will my clients notice a difference?

They keep emailing or uploading like before. The only change is friendlier reminders when something is missing.

What if it gets a document wrong?

Anything DeskTask isn't sure about goes to a review list for your team. Nothing is filed silently when confidence is low.
