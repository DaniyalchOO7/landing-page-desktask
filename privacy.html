Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

1. Who this covers

This policy explains how NexoraAI LLC ("we", "us") handles information in connection with DeskTask, our document-intake service for insurance agencies, and our website at desktask.co.

There are two kinds of information, and our role differs for each:

  • Agency data: documents and client information that an insurance agency uploads into its DeskTask account. We handle this on the agency's behalf and under its instructions. The agency decides what to upload and is responsible for its own notices to, and obligations toward, its clients.
  • Our own data: information about the people who contact us, sign up, or use DeskTask accounts, such as names, work emails, and billing details.

If you are a client of an agency and have a question about your information, please contact that agency first. We will help the agency respond.

2. What we collect

  • Signup and contact: name, agency name, work email, and phone number if you give it, through our signup form or by email.
  • Account details: usernames, email addresses, hashed passwords (we never store passwords in readable form), and two-factor authentication settings.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We receive your plan, payment status, billing email, and the last four digits of a card, but we do not store full card numbers.
  • Documents and extracted details: the files you upload (PDFs, scans, photos), the text read from them, and the details extracted, such as client names, dates, policy and coverage information, and document types. These may include sensitive personal information of your clients.
  • Activity records: an audit log of actions in your account (sign-ins, uploads, filing, exports, setting changes) and basic technical logs needed to run and secure the Service (the application itself does not record IP addresses, but our servers and hosting providers may keep standard technical logs).
  • Website: our public site is a static page. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: read documents, extract details, file and search them, answer questions about them, send reminders you set up, and show usage against your plan.
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse, and keep records of account activity.
  • To bill you, support you, and communicate about your account.
  • To improve the product using your feedback and aggregate usage counts. We do not use your documents or client data to train AI models, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

4. AI processing and sensitive numbers

DeskTask uses Google's Gemini API to read documents and answer questions. Please read this section carefully, because it affects what you tell your clients.

  • Scans and photos. When a document is a scan, photo, or image-based PDF with no readable text, the file itself is sent to Google so its text can be transcribed. Information in the image, including numbers such as Social Security numbers, can be seen by that service during transcription. We cannot mask what is only visible in an image.
  • Text. Before extracted text is sent for classification, search, or question answering, the Service replaces recognized patterns such as Social Security numbers, payment card numbers, and driver's license numbers with placeholders, and restores them in the answer shown to you. This is a safeguard, not a guarantee: it can miss unusual formats, and policy numbers are not masked because the Service needs them.
  • Paid service. We use Google's paid Gemini API tier. Under Google's published terms for paid use, content sent to the paid API is not used to train Google's models (see Google's Gemini API terms).
  • Accuracy. AI can make mistakes. See our Terms of Service.

6. How we protect information

  • Each agency has its own separate installation and database, with its own encryption key.
  • Sensitive fields are encrypted in the database. Backups are encrypted again before they leave the server. Connections to the Service use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Accounts lock after repeated failed sign-ins and support two-factor authentication. Sessions time out when idle.
  • Account activity is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the founder and any authorized personnel bound by confidentiality.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we confirm a security incident affecting your data, we will notify the affected Agency without undue delay, and within 72 hours of confirming it with what we know and what we are doing. Agencies may have their own legal duties to notify clients or regulators.

7. How long we keep information

  • Original files: deleted automatically 30 days after the document is filed. Documents waiting for review are kept until filed. This setting can be changed by the Agency.
  • Extracted details and searchable text: kept in your account until you delete them or the account ends.
  • After cancellation: you have 30 days to export your data. After that, we delete the Agency's installation and its data within 30 days.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in encrypted backups until they rotate out, within about 35 days.
  • Audit and billing records: billing and tax records are kept for up to 7 years, or longer if the law requires. An Agency's audit log is deleted along with that Agency's installation.

8. Your choices and rights

Agencies can export their data, change retention settings, and ask us to delete their account at any time by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as to access, correct, or delete it. Send requests to the email below. If your information is in an agency's files, we will pass your request to the agency, because it decides how that data is used.

9. Who this is for

DeskTask is for businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make a material change, we will tell account owners by email or in the Service before it takes effect. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
